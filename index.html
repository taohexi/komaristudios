The Studio

Meaningful impact, lovingly crafted.

Komari Studios is an independent creative studio focused on mindful art, design, and storytelling.

Our name comes from 小鞠, komari, a small ball. In the Japanese temari tradition, thread is wound around a simple core, layer by layer, until something humble becomes something treasured. We think of our home planet the same way: one small sphere, wrapped in many cultures, held together by common threads.

We carry that spirit forward: art, design, and storytelling shaped with the same patience, care, and belief that small acts of making can hold tremendous joy.
